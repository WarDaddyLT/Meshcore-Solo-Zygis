The four icons in KitCarouselIcons.h were rasterized into 32x32 and 48x48
monochrome bitmaps by AIDAS9. These bitmaps are copied without changes to the
pixel data from the AIDAS9 source at commit 3134ba4e.
https://github.com/ksk-lizdeika/MeshCore/tree/3134ba4e/examples/companion_radio/ui-new

The six supplemental icons were rendered by Žygis from the Font Awesome Free
6.7.2 source. They were centered and converted to one-bit 32x32 and 48x48
bitmaps for the kit.
https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/6.7.2/svgs/solid
https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/blob/6.7.2/svgs/brands/bluetooth-b.svg
